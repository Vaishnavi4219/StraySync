WHY STRAYSYNC?

Turning concern
into meaningful action.

StraySync is a community-focused platform designed to bring citizens, municipal officers, and rescue staff together.

From injured animals and abandoned young animals to shelter concerns and public-safety situations, our goal is to make reporting and follow-up more organized, transparent, and accountable.
